## 1. What changed on the tree (three commits)

**`16608ce` — the shove is a number the suite chooses.** PHYS-1 §11.3 measured the walking bot
delivering 0.66–3.30 m/s into the row across six runs of one build; the chain came out 2/5–5/5 with
every other bar stable to two decimals. `--phys-shove` hands REACH-1's `ServerNudgeLoose` a velocity
the suite chooses; it wakes nothing by contact and computes no impulse, so the `[phys] wake` count
stays the evidence that P1 ran — every box after the first is woken by the shipped contact path.
TailBot and PhysBot's walk are gone; PhysBot holds a crate six metres west for bars (1) and (6).

**`152a44b` — a shove that draws its own spin is still a coin flip.** Found by reading the funnel,
not by six more runs: `Carryable.Release(Vector3)` draws ±2 rad/s per axis, a box goes over at
~4 rad/s, so the draw alone decided whether a shoved box toppled, slid or fell backwards. The
overload names the spin; the two existing callers get `null` and the old behaviour.

**`d8f024b` — a can standing on its end does not roll, it skids.** Arithmetic before any run:
tipping a can needs μ > r/h = 0.58 and tin's is 0.25, so PHYS-1's 3.32 m "roll" was a can the bot
had knocked over first. `--phys-shove` grew an optional angular triple — all three or none, because a
partial triple that quietly became `(wx, 0, 0)` would stage a different event under the same command
line — and the suite spends it twice on one prop.

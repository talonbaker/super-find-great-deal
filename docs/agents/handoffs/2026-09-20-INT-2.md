## 2. Ruling 4 — one constant, and where it went

`Acceleration` 9 → 25, **in `BrowsePace.Tuning`, not in `MotorTuning.Default`**, and that is an
in-line reading of the ruling rather than a deviation from it: the constant moves from 9 to 25 in
the tuning this game actually runs.

`MotorTuning.Default` is MOVE-8's ruling — Talon at a keyboard on 2026-08-28 — and the knob table
**pins this very row** to a sprint-ramp bracket of 7.855–15.75 measured at 9. Moving the default
would delete a record rather than tune this game, and 25 is outside that bracket by design because
the supermarket has no sprint at all. `Validate` passes it: the knob's own bounds are [1, 40], and
a pin is a statement about the DEFAULT, not a ceiling on what a game may apply. It is FEEL-1's own
decision one commit earlier (*"the supermarket states its own body; the foundation keeps its"*)
applied to a second row.

**The arithmetic.** At the browse pace the ramp is 2.4 / 25 = **0.096 s** and the stop is
2.4 / 21 = **0.114 s** — inside the packet's 0.15 s bar and, for the first time, no slower than the
stop. It is 25 rather than "whatever makes 0.15 s" because the constant is not a function of the
top speed: solving 2.4 / a = 0.15 gives 16, which puts the ramp back OVER the stop the moment
`--walk-speed` moves. 25 holds the ordering across the whole `SanitizeWalkSpeed` window, and the
new theory checks both ends of it.

**+4 tests, one renamed, none loosened.** `BrowsePaceTests`' whole-record assertion goes from two
moved rows to three (the assertion IS the record, so a quiet fourth edit is still a red),
`TheRampToSpeed_IsUnderTheBar_AndNeverSlowerThanTheStop` (3 cases), and
`TheFoundationsOwnRamp_IsUnmoved`, which is the red anybody gets for moving the default instead of
the game's body.

**And it was A/B'd rather than argued — see §5**, because the first suite run after it made the

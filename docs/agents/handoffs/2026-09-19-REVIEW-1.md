### C1 — a refusal is an edge, so deliver it as one

`HideSeekState.Refusal` is true for exactly one sim tick by design, and `ApplyRound` raised no
event for it: it returned early whenever the phase had not moved. The only delivery left was
`RoundStripWidget` reading `View.Refusal` as a LEVEL on `GameHud`'s 10 Hz poll, against a ~16 ms
window.

**The presses that get refused are exactly the ones that did NOT move the round**, so gating the
refusal on a transition lost precisely the cases it exists for. The Hiding buzzer has no other
channel at all — `RoundButton.PlayPressResult` answers the presser only (`if (!mine) return;`) and
the buzzer has no press behind it — so the hider was handed `HidingGraceSec` in silence, which is
the outcome that knob's own doc promises will not happen.

The event decision is extracted into engine-free `RoundViewEvents.Between` so the table lives in
`tests/unit` instead of in a comment; the driver keeps the raising. `RoundStripWidget` subscribes
to the new `HideSeekDriver.Refused` and re-resolves the subscription against the live driver,
because that node is replaced outright on a reconnect resume. No wire change — the byte was always
on the message.

**Proved able to fail.** Planting the old gating (`Refusal: phaseChanged ? now.Refusal : None`)
took the file to `Failed: 2, Passed: 7`. The other seven tests are the positive controls: every
case also pins what must NOT be raised, so a function that reported everything on every message
fails them.

### C2 — a Confirm press must not freeze a moving target

`AuditBeforeConfirm` called `PropManager.ServerAuditRest` unconditionally. That method guards only
`PropMode.Held`, and **it cannot guard on Resting** — the settle latch is its other caller and
calls it on a prop that is still Loose. So a Loose target reached `NetworkedProp.SettleToRest`,
which sets `Body.Freeze = true`, zeroes both velocities and pins the transform; a prop a metre up
passes layer 2's bounds test (eight corners inside a room's box) and its overlap test (touching
nothing), so the audit returned Good and latched it there.

Program §5b reads *"the target must be Resting, must pass layer 2, and must be grab-reachable"* —
three preconditions. The tree implemented the first as "make it Resting". The fix is the
precondition: re-audit only a target the registry already calls Resting. `PropManager.ModeOf` is
the read-only accessor that makes it expressible.

**Planted and measured**, `ReachPlantSelfTest`'s eighth case, which goes through the shipped press
seam rather than through `ServerAuditRest` (the defect was in which of the two a press reaches):

```
guard removed: VERDICT Prop_2_HighLedge (Confirm while it is still falling) prop=1002
                 modeAfterPress=Resting frozenAfterPress=True  travelledAfter=0.000m -> FAIL
               SUMMARY cases=8 failures=3 result=FAIL
guard in:      VERDICT Prop_2_HighLedge (Confirm while it is still falling) prop=1002
                 modeAfterPress=Loose   frozenAfterPress=False travelledAfter=0.092m -> PASS
               SUMMARY cases=8 failures=0 result=PASS
```

The reviewer rated this Critical "on the strength of the throw variant" and offered Important if
the window turned out to be a handful of frames. **It is not a handful of frames**: the settle
latch needs 18 consecutive slow ticks (~0.3 s at 60 Hz) *after* the prop has stopped moving, so a
thrown object is auditable-and-freezable for its entire flight. Critical stands.

## 5. The addendum's ArriveRadius item — the diagnosis is right and the PRESCRIPTION was wrong

INT-1 handed this lane a fix: raise `ScriptedCarryIntentSource.ArriveRadius` 1.2 → 1.8 m, derived
from `PropManager.GrabRange` (2.25 m), the way TASK-1 derived `FetchStandM`. **It is ruled out by
measurement**, the same way INT-1 ruled out SHELF-1's spawn race. At 1.8 m, two of the four suites
that share the constant went red on the first sweep:

| Suite | What it printed | What actually happened |
|---|---|---|
| `Run-CarryNetTest` ph.2 | `STAGING: DropC did not disconnect while holding -- it never held the ball at all` | the bot stood **1.482 m horizontally / 1.564 m in 3D** from a ball at y = 0.5 for 28 s, `heldPropId = -1`, and **the server logged nothing at all** |
| `Run-PlaceTest` | `bot D: prop 1016 holder=0 in the final sample` | D pressed 0.6 m further out, its NAMED prop fell outside the client's pickup radius, nearest-carryable ran instead, and D came away holding **1027 — a shelf product**: `place refused peer=… prop=1027 OutsideRoomBounds` |

**There are two bars between a scripted press and a held prop, and the smaller one binds.**
`SandboxAvatar.FindNearestCarryable` only ever offers a prop within `PickupRadius` — **1.5 m, in
3D, from the avatar's origin** — and `ScriptedGrabPropId` honours a named prop only inside that
same radius, because it *"narrows the choice, never widens the reach"*. Outside it the press
composes **no request**, so `PropManager.GrabRange`'s 2.25 m is unreachable by a press the client
declined to send. It also gives *"no `grab denied` line"* a second cause: a press that was made and
found nothing.

**So `ArriveRadius` stays at 1.2 m and is now derived** — from the client's 1.5 m in 3D, which is
1.41–1.48 m horizontally for a prop resting 0.2–0.5 m up, leaving 0.2 m for the settle and a frame
of prediction error. **The wedge is fixed at the press instead**: 1.49 m in 3D is *inside* the
client's radius, so the wedged bot could have grabbed and simply never asked. The brain now presses
while still walking, on the `--carry-grab-retry` cadence, as soon as the 3D distance is inside
`SandboxAvatar.PickupRadius` — referenced, not copied, so the brain's question is literally the
client's. **The walk is untouched, so no bot's resting position moves.**

## Goal

Dead time becomes onboarding time. While waiting (and while the seeker is shut in during Hiding),
the holding room shows who is in the game and the score, and gives you objects to practise picking
up, turning, placing and throwing.

## Build (authored in `HoldingRoom.tscn`)

- **Board**: a wall panel of `Label3D` rows, built the same way as CLOCK-1's `RoundClock` (share
  the panel construction; read `docs/agents/handoffs/2026-09-19-CLOCK-1.md`), readable from 4 m in
  first person: one row per human on the wire — name, role this round ("HIDES" / "SEEKS" / "—"),
  cumulative score, and during `Holding` a "NEXT: YOU HIDE / YOU SEEK" cue so the swap is never a
  surprise; a header with the round index and the phase; a footer with the last tally line
  (`R3: <hider> hid · 3 towers · found at 1:12 · <seeker> +48`, or `ended: <name> left` when
  `EndedByDisconnect`). Late joiner sees rows and header complete from one wire message (ROUND-1's
  absolute fold); the footer needs `LastTally`, which the wire already carries. **Match line** (Talon's ruling 2026-09-19, packet MATCH-1): a match is two rounds; the
  board's header shows `MATCH n · ROUND r OF 2`, and after a match the footer shows
  `HideSeekText.MatchLine` (`<name> WON 7–5` / `DRAW`) until the next Start. Read
  `docs/agents/handoffs/2026-09-19-MATCH-1.md`; the text functions exist, reuse them. **Read the
  match result and totals from the card (`LastTally`), never from `view.Scores`** — the Start
  that begins the next match zeroes the live scores while the card still holds the result.
- **Practice corner**: a low table, eight red cubes and one of each near-miss shape (SHELF-1's
  scenes), a small target box to place into, an `InteractionSlot` pad (CARRY-1's snap) so a player
  learns that placing can snap. These are `NetworkedProp`s like everything else; they reset on
  `ResetRequested` (ROUND-1's edge) to their authored transforms so the room is tidy every round.
- The rack and START from BTN-1 stay where BTN-1 put them; you may move them if the room layout
  needs it — say so.
- A `Label3D` sign with the loop in five lines (from CLAUDE.md "The game"), positioned where a
  first-person player spawns facing it.

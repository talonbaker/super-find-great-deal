## Goal

The jack-in-the-box. Both players know the door is the only way the round ends; neither knows
when. When it goes, it has to feel physical and sudden to the hiders and nobody may lose input.

## Build

### The door is round state, not a node with RPCs

`BurstDoor` (authored in `TaskRoom.tscn`, replacing BASE-1's solid wall between the task room and
the vestibule): frame, a leaf (`Node3D` hinge + mesh), a `StaticBody3D` blocker. It has **no `[Rpc]`
methods**. It subscribes to the round wire on every peer: when the wire says the round entered
`Together` at server tick T, every peer plays the same staging from its own copy of T (the wire is
absolute, so a late joiner sees the door already open). The server also drops the blocker on its
own copy. Nothing about "open" is requested by a client.

### Staging (from §5), all knobs in one file `scripts/game/round/StartleTuning.cs` + a JSON
overlay Talon can edit without a rebuild (mirror how `MotorTuningFile` loads; `--startle-file`)

1. `TellSec` (0.4; 0 = no tell): the task-room light dips 40 % and the intercom bus plays a 0.2 s
   click + room-tone breath on the **hiders'** clients only.
2. **Burst**: leaf tween to 110° in 0.12 s with an overshoot to 125° and a 0.25 s settle (tween,
   not a hinge joint — joints tunnel); the blocker collision disabled on the same frame on every
   peer; `BurstImpulse` (server only) applies an outward impulse to every Loose/Resting prop within
   `BurstRadiusM` (3.0) of the doorway, magnitude falling off linearly (`BurstImpulseNs` 6), waking
   Resting props into Loose so the stream carries the tumble; `CameraKick` on hiders (a 0.3 s
   rotational kick of `KickDegrees` 4 with a decaying sine, on the first-person camera — FP-1's
   camera exposes yaw/pitch; add a transient offset, never write the replicated aim); bang: one
   positional shot at the doorway (`AudioStreamPlayer3D`) and one flat shot on the hiders' bus
   (`BangFlatDb` -3), synthesized (the foundation ships no audio assets; `SfxLab` has the synth).
   `ForceDropOnBurst` (true): the server releases the hider's held prop into Loose at the burst tick
   (the flinch) — through `PropManager`'s existing release funnel.
3. The seeker was teleported to the vestibule at the Found tick by ROUND-1; they walk in under their
   own control. Verify the vestibule teleport lands them facing the door.
4. **Reset**: on `ResetRequested` the leaf closes (0.6 s tween) and the blocker re-enables on every
   peer.

Optional knobs, implemented but default off, one paragraph each in the handoff on what they would
need to be tuned: `FakeKnockRate` (per minute, during Seeking, a leaf rattle + thud on the hiders'
side; server-seeded so every peer hears the same knock), `SeekerKnockKey` (the seeker can press a
key in the search room to rattle the door remotely — a taunt), `SeekerHeatOnIntercom` (hider-only
intercom crackle whose gain follows the seeker's distance to the target, server-computed, sent as
one byte on the round wire).

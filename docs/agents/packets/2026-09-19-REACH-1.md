## Goal

Layer 2: no prop ever rests inside static geometry, inside another prop, out of bounds, or below the
floor — the server corrects it or returns it to its last good transform. Layer 3: the round cannot
enter Seeking unless a seeker could, in principle, walk up to the target and grab it or dig it out
from under movable things. Both server-side; both say why when they act.

## Build

### Layer 2 — rest audit (server)

Hook the Resting latch in `PropManager` (and the kill-plane path). On latch, run
`PlacementIntegrity.Check` at the rest transform. Failure path, in order:
1. Depenetrate: query the minimum translation (Godot `PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D.CollideShape` /
   `GetRestInfo` on the prop's shape) and move the prop out along it, up to `DepenetrateMaxM`
   (0.15), re-check; if it passes, latch there and broadcast the corrected Resting transform
   (the existing funnel; clients already lerp to Resting).
2. Otherwise teleport to `LastGoodTransform` (never to spawn) and latch; log one line naming the
   prop id, the reason (`StaticOverlap | PropOverlap | OutOfBounds | KillPlane`) and both transforms.
Cost budget: one shape query per settle event; measure and report queries/second with 150 props
being knocked around by two bots.

### Layer 3 — grab-reachability (server, engine-free rule + one engine adapter)

`Reachability.Evaluate(target, sampler) → (bool reachable, ReachReason)` as an engine-free rule over
an interface the adapter fills with physics queries, so the rule itself is xUnit-testable:
- Sample `N` (12) standing points on a ring of radius `GrabReachM` (CARRY-1's grab range + hand
  reach) around the target, plus the ring at half radius. For each: downward ray finds walkable floor
  within 1.5 m; a player capsule cast confirms a body fits there.
- From each valid point's eye height (`AvatarProportions.EyeHeightM`) cast a ray at the target's
  centre and at the top of its bounds. The point counts if the **first hit is the target or a
  movable prop** (a `NetworkedProp` body). A first hit on static geometry does not count.
- Reachable ⇔ at least one point counts. Reasons: `InsideStatic` (layer 2 also failed),
  `NoStandingPoint` (too high, or boxed in by static), `OccludedByStatic`.
Register it as the `TargetRetrievable` fact source for ROUND-1 (re-evaluated when the target latches
Resting and once more on the Confirm press; never every tick). Broadcast the reason with the refusal
so BTN-1's Confirm button and the HUD strip can show "Nobody could reach that — move it".

Layer 2 keeps running during Seeking. If the target fails layer 2 during Seeking and layer 3 then
fails at the corrected/last-good transform, log it loudly (`REACH-1 target unreachable during
Seeking`) — this is the case the design must never hit, and the log line is how a playtest proves it
did not.

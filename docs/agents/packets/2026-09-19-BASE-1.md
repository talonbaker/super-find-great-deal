### 4. The world

Author `scenes/game/world/supermarket/Supermarket.tscn` — three boxy rooms, each its own section scene
instanced into it, spaced 40 m apart along +X so nothing in one is visible or audible (24 m voice
cutoff) from another:

- `HoldingRoom.tscn` — 10 × 10 × 3.5 m box: floor, four walls, ceiling, one OmniLight3D. Named
  `Marker3D` spawn points `HoldingSpawn_0` … `HoldingSpawn_3`.
- `SearchRoom.tscn` — 14 × 10 × 3.5 m box, same construction. `SearchSpawn_0`, `SearchSpawn_1`.
- `TaskRoom.tscn` — 10 × 10 × 3.5 m box. `TaskSpawn_0` … `TaskSpawn_3`, plus a 3 × 3 × 3.5 m
  vestibule attached to one wall with a solid wall between them for now (DOOR-1 replaces that wall
  with the burst door) and `Vestibule_0` inside it.

All geometry is authored in the `.tscn` (a `StaticBody3D` per wall with a `BoxShape3D` and a
`MeshInstance3D` with a `BoxMesh`; flat grey `StandardMaterial3D` with a different tint per room so a
capture says which room it is). Nothing built in code — `.claude/rules/godot-scenes.md` binds.
`Transform3D` rows, not columns.

`scripts/game/world/SupermarketWorld.cs : IGameWorld` returns the holding-room spawn points as the
default spawns and exposes the other named markers by name (`SpawnPointsFor("search")`, etc.) for
ROUND-1. Register `"supermarket"` in `Gameplay.BuildWorld` and make it the **default** `--world` in
`LaunchOptions` (and whatever the Host menu forwards to the child server). Delete the other world ids.

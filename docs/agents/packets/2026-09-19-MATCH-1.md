## Goal

Two people sit down, each gets a turn at hiding and a turn at seeking, and at the end of the
second round the game says who won. Then they can go again. No menus, no lobby: the same Start
button starts the next match.

## Build (engine-free first; one adapter; text)

- `HideSeekTuning.MatchRounds` = 2 (a knob, not a constant; `MinTimerSec`-style floor of 1).
- **Match end**: at `CommitTally`, when the round just ended is the last of its match
  (`RoundIndex % MatchRounds == 0`, with `RoundIndex` counted from 1 as today), the tally carries
  `MatchOver = true` and a `MatchResult` (`WinnerPeerId`, 0 for a draw; both totals as they stand
  after this commit; `MatchIndex = RoundIndex / MatchRounds`). Engine-free in `HideSeekTally`.
- **The wire**: add `MatchOver` (one byte) and `WinnerPeerId` (int) to `HideSeekWireTally`,
  absolute like everything else; bump the round wire's version constant if one exists (say what
  you did). Totals are already on the wire as `Scores`. Late joiner: complete from one message.
- **Tally length**: when `MatchOver`, the Tally phase lasts `MatchTallySec` (10; new knob) instead
  of `TallySec` (6) so the result can be read.
- **New match on Start**: the next `Holding → Hiding` after a `MatchOver` tally starts a new match:
  `Scores` reset to zero rows for everyone present, `RoundIndex` keeps counting (round 3 is match 2
  round 1 — the card shows the match number). Roles keep alternating as today, so the player who
  sought last hides first in the new match; state that in the handoff.
- **Text** (`HideSeekText`, engine-free, unit-tested):
  - strip line during Tally, round 1 of a match: `ROUND 1 OF 2 · <hider> hid · 3 sorted · found at
    1:12 · <seeker> +48` (use whatever the count field is named on your base; TASK-1 renames it).
  - strip line during Tally, match over: `MATCH 1 · <name> WINS 7–5` or `MATCH 1 · DRAW 6–6`;
    `ended: <name> left` still wins when `EndedByDisconnect`.
  - strip line during Holding after a match: `MATCH 1 · <name> WON 7–5 · START FOR MATCH 2`.
  - a `MatchLine(view)` beside `StripLine` for the clock (CLOCK-1) and board (HOLD-1) to reuse.
- `RoundStripWidget` shows those lines (it already renders `StripLine`; the change is in the text
  function, not the widget, unless a width change is unavoidable — say so).
- The disconnect path: a round ended by disconnect still counts toward the match; a match whose
  second round ended by disconnect is over with the totals as they stand.
